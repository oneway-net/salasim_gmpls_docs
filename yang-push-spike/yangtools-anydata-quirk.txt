Reading of XmlParserStream.read(): after an anydata, readAnyXmlValue() leaves the reader where a following
element tag is mis-consumed unless a character event follows. The publisher therefore appends a whitespace text
node after every anydata it writes (YangPushPrototype). Upstream bug to report; remove the workaround when fixed.
Same quirk: anydata followed by a sibling element in the SAME parent fails with stream readers too (AnyProbe).
